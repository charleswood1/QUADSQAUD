The Four Pillars of QUAD SQUAD

We equip believers to carry out the Great Commission through intentional, life-on-life relationships built on four foundational elements.

Encouragement

Walk alongside others in a supportive environment. Share burdens, celebrate victories, and spur one another on toward love and good deeds.

Instruction

Dive deep into God's Word together. Learn sound doctrine and practical application to build a strong, unshakeable faith foundation.

Tools

Access proven resources, study guides, and practical methodologies designed to make disciple-making approachable and effective.

Mentoring

Experience life-on-life investment. Learn from those who have gone before you and prepare to invest into those coming after you.
